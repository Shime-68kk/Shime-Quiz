OLED acting system

Eleven launch-only expressions with different silhouettes, eye mass, gaze direction, and emotional intent. Every state remains readable without color or motion.

01 · ENTRY

Turbo excited

Large inward chevrons and edge sparks. Energetic and eager, not angry.

02 · FIRST IMPACT

Impact shock

Full X shapes compress the whole face into a single collision reaction.

03 · REBOUND

Dizzy spiral

Two held spirals remain legible through the airborne rebound and landing.

04 · RECOVERY

Annoyed

Heavy inward lids create a low, irritated stare before the leg deployment.

05 · APPROACH

Determined

Focused pupils lock onto the pillar while the inner brows angle down.

06 · AFTER KICK

Smug delight

One pleased arc and one half-lidded eye form a deliberately asymmetric beat.

07 · RETURN

Return panic

Maximum eye area with both pupils pulled upward toward the returning pillar.

08 · SECOND IMPACT

Compressed impact

Thin horizontal eyes and a center flash communicate vertical compression.

09 · TUMBLE

Tumble dazed

Mismatched rotated shapes keep the face readable while the body spins.

10 · TITLE ASSEMBLY

Side gaze

Dark pupils shift right so the robot clearly reads the assembled title.

11 · FINAL HOLD

Final delight

Two clean upward arcs create a proud, calm smile for the two-second hold.

Silhouette ruleNo state relies on a tiny pupil adjustment alone.
OLED ruleAll paths remain high-contrast and feasible on a monochrome hardware display.
Motion ruleExpression changes reinforce a visible story beat; they do not carry meaning alone.
